Step 1 — Hardware

Before you fly, gather the following:

  • Crazyflie 2.x
  • Flow deck v2 — downward laser ranging and optical flow, used to hold position
  • Multi-ranger deck — front / back / left / right / up laser ranging, used for obstacle detection
  • Crazyradio PA
  • A charged LiPo battery

The decks mount on the Crazyflie's expansion headers: the Flow deck underneath, the Multi-ranger deck on top.

Step 2 — Assembly

  1. Mount the Flow deck v2 underneath the Crazyflie, connector aligned with the expansion header.
  2. Mount the Multi-ranger deck on top, with its four side sensors facing front, back, left, and right.
  3. Check that each propeller is on the correct motor — mixed-up propellers will flip the drone on takeoff.
  4. Charge the LiPo battery fully before flight.

The Flow deck needs a textured, well-lit surface to hold position. Bare glossy floors will not work.

Step 3 — Firmware

CaveBat runs custom firmware that must be flashed once from a PC over a Crazyradio — stock firmware cannot fly a mission.

  1. Install the ARM toolchain — see Bitcraze's documentation.
  2. Put the drone in bootloader mode: hold the power button for about 3 seconds until the LEDs change pattern.
  3. Flash the firmware with cfloader:
python3 -m cfloader flash cf2.bin stm32-fw

After any Crazyradio session, power-cycle the drone before it will accept a Bluetooth connection.

Step 4 — Using the app

  1. Connect to the drone over Bluetooth from the CaveBat app.
  2. Run through the hardware checklist — each tick confirms a deck or sensor responded and is ready.
  3. Set a hover timer and altitude for the mission.
  4. Use the abort button any time to cut the mission short.

Always test with propellers removed first. Fly only in an open space with at least 2m of clearance on all sides.
